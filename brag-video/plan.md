# ARTHA (OpenWA) — 60-SECOND LAUNCH VIDEO PRODUCTION PLAN

## 1. PROJECT SUMMARY
- **Product**: Artha (OpenWA) — Free, Open Source WhatsApp API Gateway & AI Orchestration Platform
- **Target Audience**: Developers, Software Engineers, Founders, and Tech Businesses seeking reliable WhatsApp messaging infrastructure without Meta Cloud API vendor lock-in or per-conversation fees.
- **Duration**: Exactly 60.0 Seconds (1,800 frames @ 30fps)
- **Output Spec**: 1920x1080 Full HD, H.264 video (High Profile, YUV420p), AAC 48kHz Stereo Audio, 30fps MP4.

## 2. CREATIVE DIRECTION & ART DIRECTION
- **Tone**: Cinematic, Developer-First, Authoritative Tech Launch ("Apple Keynote meets Linear/Stripe engineering trailer").
- **Color Palette**:
  - Background: Deep Void Slate (`#080C14` to `#0B1320`)
  - Accent Primary: WhatsApp Emerald Neon (`#25D366` / `#00E676`)
  - Accent Secondary: Cyan Glow (`#00F2FE` / `#4FACFE`)
  - Text Primary: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Text Secondary: Cool Silver / Muted Slate (`#94A3B8`)
  - Glass Card Fill: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and subtle `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)` border.
- **Typography**: Inter / Outfit / System UI with bold geometric weights (700/800 for hooks, 500 for captions, monospace for code).

## 4. SOUND DESIGN SPECIFICATION
- **Voiceover**: Neural Studio TTS (`en-US-ChristopherNeural`, calibrated pace, authoritative and clear).
- **Background Music**: 120 BPM driving cinematic synthwave/electronic background track with crisp sub-bass kicks, hi-hats, and synth pads.
- **Audio Ducking**: Music automatically ducks -10dB when speech is active, maintaining perfect speech intelligibility.
- **Sound Effects**:
  - Sub-bass impact at 0.0s, 7.5s, 51.0s
  - Digital data whooshes at scene transitions
  - Terminal typing sounds and UI success chimes at 24s and 35s
  - Final deep cinematic boom at 57s.
