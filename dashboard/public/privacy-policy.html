Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Artha is a business messaging and automation app. Businesses use it to manage their WhatsApp, Instagram professional account and Facebook Page: publishing posts, replying to comments and messages, and keeping a record of those conversations. This policy explains what information Artha handles and how.

Information we receive

  • From Instagram and Facebook (through Meta's official APIs): when you comment on, or message, an account connected to Artha, we receive your public user ID and username, and the text of your comment or message.
  • From WhatsApp: your phone number, profile name and the messages you exchange with the business.
  • From the business using Artha: account details, access tokens for their connected accounts, and the content they publish.

How we use it

  • To reply to your comments and messages, either automatically or by the business's staff.
  • To publish posts the business has created or scheduled.
  • To show the business a log of comments, replies and messages.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your information is shared only with the business you interacted with, and with the service providers needed to run Artha (hosting and Meta's platform). We disclose information if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers. Access tokens are kept on the server and never shown again after they are saved. We keep comment and message records only as long as needed to provide the service, and at most 12 months, unless the law requires longer.

Deleting your data

To ask for your data to be deleted:

  1. Send a direct message to the Instagram account or Facebook Page you interacted with, saying "Delete my data".
  2. Include your Instagram or Facebook username.

We delete your comments, messages and identifiers from Artha within 30 days and confirm once it is done. You can also remove Artha's access to your own account at any time in Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites.

Children

Artha is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

For any privacy question, message the Instagram account or Facebook Page you interacted with, and the business will reply through Artha.
